## Ten questions

- [ ] **1. Does the first screen say what you do?**
      Before scrolling, is it clear that you advise people, and on what?

- [ ] **2. Does it look like one thing all the way through?**
      The same colours, the same lettering, the same feel on every page.

- [ ] **3. Does your eye land where it should?**
      On each page, does the most important thing stand out first?

- [ ] **4. Could this be anyone's website, or does it feel like yours?**
      If you covered your name, would it still feel like your practice?

- [ ] **5. The drawing of you.**
      It appears on nearly every page. Is it right? Would you rather have a
      photograph instead, later?

- [ ] **6. How it moves.**
      Things fade in gently as you scroll, and answers slide open when tapped.
      Too much, too little, or about right?

- [ ] **7. Is it comfortable to read?**
      Especially on your phone. Nothing too small, nothing too cramped.

- [ ] **8. Show it to someone who has not seen it.**
      Give them five seconds on the first screen, then ask: what is this site,
      who is it for, and what would you click? Their answer tells us more than
      almost anything else.

- [ ] **9. Can everyone use it?**
      We have checked this with tools — no small grey text, everything works by
      keyboard, no flashing. Tell us if anything is hard to see or use.

- [ ] **10. The words on the front page.**
      They are written in your voice, in the first person:

      > "People come to me when a decision will not settle — a job offer, a
      > marriage, the right time to move. We talk it through, with your chart as
      > a starting point, not a verdict."

      **This is the most important one.** It is published under your name. If it
      does not sound like you, say so and we will change it.
