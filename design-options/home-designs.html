Questions

Before you reach out

How do consultations take place — online or in person?
Most consultations are arranged remotely, so you can take part comfortably from wherever you are. Reach out by WhatsApp, phone, or the contact form and we’ll find a time that suits you.
What details will I need to provide?
It depends on the service. A Vedic birth-chart reading needs your date, time, and place of birth; a Vastu report is helped by photographs or a simple floor plan of your space.
Is this a substitute for medical, legal, or financial advice?
No. This guidance is offered for reflection and wellbeing, and is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial advice. You remain free in every decision you make.
I’m completely new to this — is that okay?
Absolutely. Many people come with little or no prior knowledge. There’s no pressure and no judgement — only a calm, respectful conversation about what you’re seeking.
