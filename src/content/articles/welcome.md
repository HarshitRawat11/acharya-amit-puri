This is a **sample article**. It is hidden (`draft: true`) until you're ready.

To add your own writing later, copy this file, give it a new name (for example
`finding-balance-at-home.md`), update the details at the top, and write your
piece below. No coding required.

You can use simple formatting:

- **Bold** and _italic_ text
- Lists like this one
- [Links](https://example.com) and headings

When you'd like it to appear on the site, change `draft: true` to `draft: false`.
