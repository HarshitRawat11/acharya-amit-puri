# INTENT BRIEF — Acharya Amit Puri

## Audience

**Urban Indian professionals facing a decision that will not settle** — a job
offer, a career turn, whether and when to marry, whether this is the right year
to move. They are educated, sceptical of anything that feels like fortune-telling,
and would be quietly embarrassed to be seen on an astrology site that looked like
one. They are not looking for a prediction. They are looking for someone
thoughtful to help them think.

This is **not** the property-owner audience the live site currently addresses.

## Core message

> When a decision will not settle, Acharya Amit Puri will sit with it properly —
> reading your chart as a starting point, never as a verdict.

## Primary action

**Book a Consultation** — one action, on every page.

⚠️ **This action currently cannot complete.** Phone, WhatsApp, email and the
enquiry form are all unfilled placeholders, recorded in `FINISH-LINE.md` §1.1 as
owed by the client. An intent brief whose primary action is unreachable is a brief
describing a site that cannot yet do its job. Recorded here, not resolved here.

## Desired first impression, in five seconds

> *"A real person who will take my question seriously — and who I could message
> without feeling foolish."*

Specifically, within five seconds a visitor should register: a **named individual**
(not an agency), a **calm, non-mystical** tone, and an **obvious, low-commitment
way to start a conversation.**

## Benchmarks

Not competitors. Each was opened and checked on 2026-09-19 — VERIFIED live.

**1. `wizardzines.com`** — the closest structural analogue anywhere: a hand-drawn
recurring character *is* the brand, speaking to you in the first person from a
warm ground, with no photography at all. Learn the mechanism — one illustrated
figure that appears on every page and does the talking — while rejecting its
register entirely, which is far too playful for a practitioner.

**2. `jessicahische.is`** — opens with a single first-person sentence on warm
cream, and lets illustrated work fill the whole visual field instead of a portrait.
Learn how one plain sentence in the first person ("I'm a lettering artist…")
establishes a person faster than any amount of biography, and how warmth can come
from the ground colour rather than from photography.

**3. `oliverburkeman.com`** — calm, plain, first-person writing about genuinely
weighty personal subjects, with no mysticism and no urgency. Learn the register:
how to be serious about mortality, limits and hard choices while still sounding
like a person you could email. Note it does use a photograph, which this project
has excluded for v1.

## What choosing "Warm · Grounded · Approachable" commits us to

These follow from the decision and are stated now so they are not discovered later
as surprises. **None are yet gates** — Phase D decides which become measurable.

1. **The mascot must reach mobile.** It is `hidden lg:block`, so no phone visitor
   ever sees it. Under this direction the illustrated figure is the primary warmth
   carrier, and it is currently absent for most of the audience.
2. **The voice shifts to first person.** The site speaks in the third person
   ("Acharya Amit Puri reviews everything with care"). "Warm" and "approachable"
   are carried by "I", not by "he".
3. **The copy's subject shifts from home to life decisions.** The hero currently
   leads with *"bring your home, your choices, and your nature into greater
   harmony."* The chosen audience is not primarily asking about their home.
4. **The current design most closely matches Direction 1, not 3.** Expect Gate 1
   (Intent Alignment) to **FAIL on first measurement**. That is the point of
   measuring.
5. **`/services/` renders 14px body text** — the smallest on the site, on the page
   that sells. Small type reads as *corporate*, an anti-adjective.
